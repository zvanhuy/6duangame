HUONG DAN HIERARCHY VA AM THANH - GAME RAN

1. Hierarchy da duoc tach thanh cac nhom:
- 00_CAMERA: chua Camera Chinh.
- 01_BOARD_NEN_TUONG: chua nen ban choi, vung choi va 4 buc tuong.
- 02_SNAKE_RAN: khi Play se chua Dau ran va Than ran.
- 03_FOOD_MOI: khi Play se chua Moi.
- 04_UI_GIAO_DIEN: khi Play se chua Canvas, man hinh bat dau, man hinh ket thuc va diem.
- 05_AUDIO: chua AudioSource dung de phat am thanh.
- 99_GAME_MANAGER: chua object Trinh quan ly tro choi ran va script QuanLyTroChoiRan.cs.

2. File am thanh da them:
- Assets/Resources/Audio/an_moi.wav: phat khi ran an moi.
- Assets/Resources/Audio/chet.wav: phat khi ran chet do cham tuong hoac can than.

3. Cho code da sua trong QuanLyTroChoiRan.cs:
- Resources.Load<AudioClip>("Audio/an_moi")
- Resources.Load<AudioClip>("Audio/chet")
- PhatAmThanh(amAnMoi, amLuongAnMoi) khi an moi.
- PhatAmThanh(amChet, amLuongChet) khi game over.

4. Neu muon thay am thanh:
- Giu dung ten file an_moi.wav va chet.wav.
- Dat file moi vao Assets/Resources/Audio/.
- Xoa file cu hoac ghi de file cu.
- Mo lai Unity de import lai am thanh.
